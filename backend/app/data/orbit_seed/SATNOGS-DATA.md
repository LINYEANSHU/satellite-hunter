# SatNOGS orbital snapshots

The weather.json and amateur.json datasets contain selected SatNOGS DB TLE records, including the original TLE lines and their listed upstream source. The snapshots are attributed to SatNOGS DB contributors and Libre Space Foundation.

Satellite Hunter filtered records by published transmitter service and weather spacecraft identifiers, added a few cross-referenced AMSAT display names, converted display fields to OMM-style names, retained original TLEs for propagation, and kept the latest usable epoch for each NORAD ID. The adaptations are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
